About

Why 2026 Changes Everything

Formula 1 enters one of its biggest resets. New chassis and power unit regulations bring smaller, lighter cars, active aerodynamics and a far greater reliance on electrical power.

The grid also grows to eleven teams, with Cadillac joining as a new constructor and Audi taking over the former Sauber entry.

⚡ Electric Power

Roughly half of the power now comes from the electric side, with 100% sustainable fuels.

🌀 Active Aero

Movable front and rear wings replace DRS to cut drag on straights.

🏁 Lighter Cars

Shorter wheelbase, narrower floor and reduced weight for nimbler racing.

🆕 New Names

Audi and Cadillac arrive, while Ford partners with Red Bull and Honda joins Aston Martin.
